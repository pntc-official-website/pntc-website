About the App

Level up your maritime career without the boredom

PNTC Maritime Review brings maritime exam preparation to your phone through gamified digital learning. It joins traditional maritime education with technology and turns licensure preparation into an engaging experience you can see progress in.

Board exam questions become a digital progression system. Instead of passive memorization, you build active mastery and study consistently, anytime and anywhere.

  • Made for MARINA examinations
  • Experience points, badges and daily streaks
  • Stats that show where you need more work
  • Free to start on Android

Features

Gamified to the core

A full progression system turns repetitive board exam review into something you want to come back to every day.

01

Earn Experience Points

Every question you answer fills your progress bar. Watch your points add up and unlock rare titles.

02

Badges and Achievements

Collect badges from First Step, for your first completed competence, up to Streak Legend, for 180 days of review without a miss.

03

Daily Review Streaks

Answer 20 questions a day to earn points and keep your review streak going.

04

Daily Mock Test and Retake

Take a daily mock test of random questions, then retake the ones you got wrong until they stick.

05

Stats That Make You Smarter

See your weakest areas, follow your accuracy over time and look back on your best performance.

06

Exam Guidelines Built In

Choose the exam you plan to take and get its official guidelines right inside the app.
