PNTC Maritime Training and Assessment Center is one of the Philippines' premier institutions for maritime education and training. We are fully accredited by MARINA (Maritime Industry Authority) and compliant with the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping (STCW).

Whether you are a new cadet starting your maritime career or an experienced officer renewing your certifications, our state-of-the-art training facilities and experienced maritime instructors will prepare you for safe and successful service at sea.

Programs Offered

Our STCW Courses

Internationally recognized certifications required for seafarers. All courses are conducted by MARINA-accredited instructors and assessors.

STCW Basic Safety
Basic Safety Training (BST)
The foundational maritime safety certification required for all seafarers. Covers personal survival techniques, fire prevention, elementary first aid, and personal safety.
4 Days Enroll
STCW Regulation VI/2
Proficiency in Survival Craft & Rescue Boats (PSCRB)
Training in the operation of lifeboats, rescue boats, and marine evacuation systems. Required for officers and ratings in charge of survival craft.
3 Days Enroll
STCW Regulation VI/3
Advanced Fire Fighting (AFF)
Advanced techniques in fire fighting at sea including coordinating fire-fighting operations, shipboard fire-fighting organization, hazards and damage control.
2.5 Days Enroll
STCW Regulation VI/4-1
Medical First Aid (MFA)
Training in providing first aid aboard ships including assessment of injured persons, life-saving techniques, and using medical supplies and equipment.
3 Days Enroll
STCW Regulation VI/6
Security Awareness Training (SAT)
Mandatory training on maritime security threats and awareness, shipboard security procedures, and responding to security incidents under the ISPS Code.
1 Day Enroll
STCW Regulation V/1-1
Crowd & Crisis Management (CCM)
Training for passenger ship personnel in managing crowd situations, handling passengers in distress, and emergency response communication.
1 Day Enroll
STCW Regulation VI/2-2
Proficiency in Fast Rescue Boats (PFRB)
Specialized training in the handling and operation of fast rescue boats for search and rescue operations at sea.
2 Days Enroll
STCW Regulation VI/4-2
Medical Care (MC)
In-depth training for seafarers designated to provide medical care aboard ship, including diagnosing illness, medical treatment, and emergency response.
5 Days Enroll
