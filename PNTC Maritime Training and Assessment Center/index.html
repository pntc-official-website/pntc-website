PNTC Maritime Training and Assessment Center is one of the Philippines' premier institutions for maritime education and training. We are fully accredited by MARINA (Maritime Industry Authority) and compliant with the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping (STCW).

Whether you are a new cadet starting your maritime career or an experienced officer renewing your certifications, our state-of-the-art training facilities and experienced maritime instructors will prepare you for safe and successful service at sea.
