Who We Are

Virtue and Quality at the Heart of Everything We Do

PNTC Colleges, Inc. is a private, non-sectarian maritime and business institution supervised by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and the Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA). We serve students and professionals as a Higher Education Institution (HEI), Maritime Training Institution (MTI), and Maritime Assessment Center (AC).

Over three decades, PNTC has built one of the country's most complete maritime education ecosystems — from senior high school to professional-level seafarer certification, all under one institutional umbrella.

Our Mission

To provide quality maritime and business education, training, and assessment anchored on virtue and excellence — developing globally competent professionals who serve their industries, communities, and country with integrity.

  • Deliver world-class maritime education meeting international STCW standards
  • Cultivate professional excellence, integrity, and service among our students
  • Equip graduates with competencies demanded by global maritime and business industries
  • Provide accessible, connected pathways from secondary to professional-level education
  • Foster meaningful partnerships with the shipping industry and government agencies

Our Vision

To be the leading maritime and business institution in the Philippines and Southeast Asia — recognized for producing globally competent professionals who exemplify virtue, quality, and excellence in every field they serve.

  • Premier maritime education institution in the Asia-Pacific region
  • Internationally recognized for STCW-compliant training and fair assessment
  • A complete educational continuum from K–12 through professional certification
  • Model institution for character formation alongside academic and technical mastery

Core Values

Every program, policy, and interaction at PNTC is grounded in two foundational principles: Virtue and Quality. These are not aspirational — they are operational.

  • Virtue — Integrity, honesty, and moral courage in all conduct and decisions
  • Quality — Excellence in education, training, assessment, and institutional practice
  • Service — Commitment to our students, industry partners, and the Filipino nation
  • Discipline — Respect for structure, professional standards, and accountability
  • Excellence — Continuous improvement and the pursuit of mastery in every endeavor
