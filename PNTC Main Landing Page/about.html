About PNTC

Established in 1994, PNTC Colleges, Inc. is a private, non-sectarian maritime and business institution with campuses and facilities in Dasmariñas (Cavite), Intramuros (Manila), and Tanza (Cavite). Here you will find our institutional profile, our Board-approved vision and mission, and the mission of each of our colleges.

Who We Are

Virtue and Quality at the Heart of Everything We Do

PNTC Colleges, Inc. is a private, non-sectarian maritime and business institution supervised by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and the Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA). We serve students and professionals as a Higher Education Institution (HEI), Maritime Training Institution (MTI), and Maritime Assessment Center (AC).

Over three decades, PNTC has built a complete maritime education pathway, from senior high school to professional seafarer certification, within one institution.

Our Mission

Our mission is to educate and inspire through academic programs that develop competencies and build character to prepare our students for global success.

To achieve our mission, we are committed to the following.

  1. Dedicated TeachingTeaching with dedication through curricula, methodologies, and practices that advance students' competencies and character, while enhancing their micro-credentials and soft skills.
  2. Real-World ExperienceProviding students with real-world experience in their chosen fields through practical and training-focused final years.
  3. Accessible EducationEnsuring that our education is accessible to all by removing physical, financial, social, or other barriers.
  4. Qualified Faculty and StaffEngaging only qualified, competent, and inspiring faculty, and ensuring that our staff are competent, friendly, and supportive.
  5. A Complete College ExperienceDelivering a complete college experience with access to comprehensive services, including library resources, guidance counseling, career and alumni services, sports and arts programs, healthcare, nutrition, student organizations, safety and security, dormitories, and other facilities.
  6. A Safe, Modern EnvironmentProviding a safe, inclusive, and engaging learning environment equipped with advanced technologies.
  7. Streamlined ProcessesStreamlining registration, enrollment, payment, and other processes for the ease and convenience of our students.
  8. Rigorous ComplianceMaintaining rigorous compliance with government regulations and industry standards.
  9. Innovation and ResearchContributing to the knowledge and advancement of industries through innovation and research.

Core Values

Every program, policy, and interaction at PNTC is grounded in two principles, Virtue and Quality. We apply these principles in our daily work.

  • Virtue
    Integrity, honesty, and moral courage in all conduct and decisions
  • Quality
    Excellence in education, training, assessment, and institutional practice
  • Service
    Commitment to our students, industry partners, and the Filipino nation
  • Discipline
    Respect for structure, professional standards, and accountability
  • Excellence
    Continuous improvement and the pursuit of mastery in every endeavor

Mission Statements

Our Colleges and Basic Education

⚓

College of Maritime Education

Our mission is to educate and inspire through programs that prepare students for the global maritime industry.

The College of Maritime Education is committed to instilling discipline, developing maritime competencies, and cultivating skills, soft skills, and micro-skills to produce seafarers who survive and thrive in the global maritime industry.

Our students will gain practical experience through rigorous training in our training center, onboard our dedicated training vessel, or through partnerships with local and international maritime entities.

📦

College of Customs and Business Management Education

Our mission is to educate and inspire through programs that prepare students for global trade and business.

The College of Customs and Business Management Education is committed to instilling innovation, developing business competencies, and cultivating skills, soft skills, and micro-skills to produce professionals who drive success in global trade, medical, legal, and business operations.

Our students will gain practical experience through rigorous training in our training center, on-the-job training, startup incubators, simulation labs, or partnerships with local and international businesses.

✈️

College of International Tourism

Our mission is to educate and inspire through programs that prepare students for the global tourism and hospitality industries.

The College of International Tourism is committed to instilling professionalism, developing hospitality competencies, and cultivating skills, soft skills, and micro-skills to produce professionals who deliver exceptional services, adapt to industry challenges, and lead with integrity.

Our students will gain practical experience through rigorous training in our training center, on-the-job training, real-world hotel and restaurant training, or partnerships with local and international entities.

⚖️

College of Criminology

Our mission is to educate and inspire through programs that prepare students for the global criminal justice, law enforcement, and public safety sectors.

The College of Criminology is committed to instilling courage, developing criminology competencies, and cultivating skills, soft skills, and micro-skills to produce officers in the public safety sector who provide services in crime prevention, crime detection and investigation, and law enforcement while leading with unwavering integrity and expertise.

Our students will gain practical experience through rigorous training in our training center, on-the-job training, real world law enforcement scenarios, and partnerships with local and international entities.

📘

Basic Education Department

Our mission is to educate and inspire through programs that prepare students for success in further studies, careers, and life.

The Basic Education Department is dedicated to developing well-rounded individuals who demonstrate intellectual growth, nationalism, social competence, physical well-being, integrity, responsibility, and emotional maturity.

Vision and Mission Statements as approved by the PNTC Colleges, Inc. Board of Directors on 28 June 2025 (BOD-RES-EXT Nos. 2025-001, 2025-002, and 2025-004).

With Virtue and Quality at the heart of its policies, PNTC Colleges is a well-known maritime school and training center and a growing institution for business education.
