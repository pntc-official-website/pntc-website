Admissions

Getting In is
Easier Than You Think.

Five steps. No stress. Your PNTC SHS journey starts here.

01
Online or Walk-in

Application Form

Fill out the application form online from wherever you are, or swing by the Admission Office in person. Either way works — no pressure, no long lines.

02
Maritime & Criminology only · Free

Medical Exam

If you're going into Pre-Bac Maritime or TVL Maritime — this one's for you. A standard medical check to confirm you're fit for the program. It's free of charge, and non-maritime students can skip this step entirely.

03
On-site

Admission Exam

A short entrance exam to help us understand your strengths and match you to the right strand. Nothing to stress over — just show up and do your thing.

04
Requirements

Submission of Documents

Hand in your Grade 10 Report Card (or completion certificate), PSA Birth Certificate, and a 2x2 ID photo. Short checklist. Fast process.

05
Final Step

Enrollment

Pay your enrollment fee, confirm your strand, and lock in your slot. Seats are limited — once you're in, you're officially a PNTCian.

Academic Track

Academic Programs

7 Programs Available

PNTC's Academic Track prepares students for college and university — each program is tailored to a specific career path so you're not just earning a diploma, you're building a direction.

ADSC
Doorway to Ship's Catering
Culinary arts foundation tailored for careers in ship's catering and maritime hospitality aboard merchant vessels.
ADT
Doorway to Tourism
Foundation in hospitality, travel management, and tourism operations — ideal for degrees in Hotel and Restaurant Management or Tourism.
ADMarE
Doorway to Marine Engineering
A pre-engineering track designed to give students a strong foundation before entering BS Marine Engineering at PNTC Colleges.
ADMT
Doorway to Marine Transportation
Pre-maritime deck track for aspiring ship's officers — the direct academic path into PNTC's BS Marine Transportation program.
AESH
English, Social Sciences & Humanities
Humanities-centered track developing communication, critical thinking, and social science skills — ideal for law, communications, psychology, and education paths.
ASTEM
Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics
Rigorous STEM foundation covering physics, chemistry, biology, and advanced mathematics — preparing students for engineering, medicine, and technology degrees.
AUP
Uniformed Personnel
Structured academic track for students pursuing careers in the military, police, coast guard, fire protection, or other government uniformed services.
Technical-Professional Track

Tech-Pro Programs

2 Programs Available

The Technical-Professional Track gives students hands-on, job-ready maritime skills recognized by TESDA and STCW — opening the door to immediate employment in the maritime industry after graduation.

TPDM
Doorway to Maritime
Introductory technical maritime program covering basic seamanship, deck operations, and safety protocols — a gateway to maritime careers or advanced tech-pro studies.
Ordinary Seaman Entry-Level Maritime
TPM
Maritime (Tech-Pro)
Full technical maritime training with STCW certifications and TESDA NC qualifications. Graduates are qualified to work as Able Seamen or Engine Cadets in international shipping fleets.
Able Seaman Engine Cadet TESDA Certified STCW Certified
