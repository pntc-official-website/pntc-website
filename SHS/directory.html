Academic Track

Academic Programs

7 Programs Available

PNTC's Academic Track prepares students for college and university. Each program is designed around a specific career path.

ADSC
Doorway to Ship's Catering
Culinary arts foundation tailored for careers in ship's catering and maritime hospitality aboard merchant vessels.
ADT
Doorway to Tourism
A foundation in hospitality, travel management, and tourism operations, suited for degrees in Hotel and Restaurant Management or Tourism.
ADMarE
Doorway to Marine Engineering
A pre-engineering track designed to give students a strong foundation before entering BS Marine Engineering at PNTC Colleges.
ADMT
Doorway to Marine Transportation
A pre-maritime deck track for aspiring ship officers and the direct academic path to PNTC's BS Marine Transportation program.
AESH
English, Social Sciences & Humanities
A humanities track that develops communication, critical thinking, and social science skills, suited for studies in law, communication, psychology, and education.
ASTEM
Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics
A strong STEM foundation in physics, chemistry, biology, and advanced mathematics that prepares students for degrees in engineering, medicine, and technology.
AUP
Uniformed Personnel
Structured academic track for students pursuing careers in the military, police, coast guard, fire protection, or other government uniformed services.
Technical-Professional Track

Tech-Pro Programs

2 Programs Available

The Technical-Professional Track gives students practical maritime skills recognized by TESDA and STCW, preparing them for employment in the maritime industry after graduation.

TPDM
Doorway to Maritime
An introductory technical maritime program covering basic seamanship, deck operations, and safety protocols. It prepares students for maritime careers or advanced Tech-Pro studies.
Ordinary Seaman Entry-Level Maritime
TPM
Maritime (Tech-Pro)
Full technical maritime training with STCW certifications and TESDA NC qualifications. Graduates are qualified to work as Able Seamen or Engine Cadets in international shipping fleets.
Able Seaman Engine Cadet TESDA Certified STCW Certified
