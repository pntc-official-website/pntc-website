Why Choose PNTC?

Not Just
A Degree.
A Career.

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes, and we don't take that lightly. PNTC Colleges has spent over 30 years building its reputation in maritime education, producing graduates who have gone on to serve in international fleets, government agencies, and global industries. That track record didn't happen by accident. It came from holding our standards high, investing in real facilities, and putting the right people in front of every classroom. Today, CHED-recognized, MARINA-accredited, and ISO 9001:2015-certified, we've extended that same proven system to Tourism, Criminology, Customs Administration, and Office Administration. Because every student, in every field, deserves education built to the same level of excellence. And we've made sure it doesn't have to cost the earth to access it.

Our Story
Built on Maritime. Proven Across Every Field.

We spent 30 years mastering one thing — producing maritime graduates who are genuinely ready for what the industry demands. That same formula of discipline, rigour, and real standards now drives every program we offer. What worked at sea works everywhere.

Our Facilities
Equipped So Our Students Are Ready

Ship bridge and engine room simulators. A live training vessel. Campus swimming pools. Criminology and tourism laboratories. We make sure every student — in every program — trains in an environment that reflects the real world they are preparing for.

Our People
Deans and Heads Who Are Above Average

Our college heads and deans don't just hold the title — they bring exceptional industry experience and academic leadership to every program they run. When guidance comes from people who have lived what they teach, students feel the difference from day one.

Our Promise
Quality Education Without Breaking the Bank

CHED-recognized. MARINA-accredited. ISO 9001:2015-certified. Parents shouldn't have to choose between giving their child the best and keeping the family stable. At PNTC, they don't have to — because we've worked hard to make sure quality and affordability are not opposites.
