Enrollment Now Open for S.Y. 2026–2027

PNTC Registrar · September 9, 2026

We are excited to announce that enrollment for School Year 2026–2027 is officially open across all PNTC campuses. Whether you are planning to enroll in Senior High School, pursue a college degree, or complete maritime training, our admissions team is ready to assist you.

How to Enroll

Visit any PNTC campus during office hours, or contact us through our official channels. Bring the required documents and a valid ID.
