Bridging Education and Industry Excellence: PNTC Colleges Partners with Acacia Hotel Manila

PNTC Communications·September 11, 2026

At PNTC Colleges (CITM), our priority has always been to prepare our students for real-world success. Today, we are thrilled to announce a major milestone in that mission: PNTC Colleges has officially partnered with Acacia Hotel Manila!

This dynamic collaboration represents a powerful step forward in bridging the gap between academic learning and industry excellence. By joining forces with a premier five-star luxury hotel, we are ensuring that our community gains direct access to top-tier hospitality standards, professional mentorship, and unparalleled career opportunities.

What This Partnership Means for Our Community

This strategic alliance is built on a shared commitment to experiential learning, practical growth, and continuous development. Here is how this partnership empowers both our students and faculty:

1. Elite On-the-Job Training (OJT) for Students

Theory comes to life when applied in real-world environments. Through this partnership, PNTC Colleges students will step into the world of luxury hospitality at Acacia Hotel Manila, gaining hands-on work exposure across various hotel operations. From guest relations and front office management to culinary operations and event coordination, our students will train alongside industry-seasoned professionals, building the confidence, discipline, and skills required to stand out in a competitive job market.

2. Dynamic Training and Development for Faculty & Students

To deliver world-class education, academic instruction must continuously evolve with market demands. This partnership provides tailored training and development initiatives for both our educators and students. By keeping our faculty engaged with current hospitality trends and standards, we ensure that our classroom instruction remains relevant, cutting-edge, and directly aligned with the evolving global hospitality landscape.

3. A Clearer Pathway to Future Careers

Beyond practical training, working within a recognized luxury brand like Acacia Hotel Manila gives our graduates a distinct advantage. Experiencing high-level operations firsthand allows students to build strong professional networks early on, paving the way for smooth transitions from academic life into long-term careers.

Elevating Hospitality Education Together

This collaboration reinforces PNTC Colleges’ commitment to providing accessible, high-quality, and practical avenues for growth. By pairing rigorous academic foundations with elite industry exposure, we are preparing the next generation of hospitality leaders to excel with confidence.

Get ready to experience hospitality training at its finest! Stay tuned for more updates, student success stories, and upcoming initiatives powered by PNTC Colleges and Acacia Hotel Manila.
