Welcome to the Pack: Highlights from PNTC Colleges’ Freshman Fiesta 2026! ⚓

PNTC Communications·September 9, 2026

The campus was buzzing with energy, music, and school pride as PNTC Colleges officially welcomed the newest batch of college students at Freshman Fiesta 2026!

Whether you're stepping onto campus to chart your course on the high seas or preparing to lead in corporate and public sectors, the orientation and welcome festival set the ultimate tone for Academic Year 2026–2027.

Setting Sail: A Warm Welcome to A.Y. 2026–2027

Freshman Fiesta 2026 brought together student leaders, faculty, and new faces across all degree programs:

  • Maritime Programs: Future seafarers and engineers embarking on BS Marine Transportation and BS Marine Engineering.
  • Business & Service Programs: Aspiring leaders entering BS Criminology, BS Customs Administration, BS Tourism Management, and BS Office Administration.

The event allowed incoming freshmen to break the ice, meet their professors, explore the campus grounds, and discover the vibrant student life that awaits them.

High-Octane Festival Highlights

🎡 The Ultimate Org Fair & Booth Experience

Student organizations pulled out all the stops with interactive booths that kept everyone fueled and entertained:

Snack & Coffee Stations: Free brews and bites to keep the hype going all day.

Karaoke Hub: Freshmen belt out their favorite anthems and showcase surprise vocal talent.

Pop-Up Cinema: A chill zone for students to relax, watch short flicks, and connect with fellow batchmates.

🎤The Main Stage Showcase & Concert

The festivities wrapped up with performances by PNTC’s talented students, turning the campus courtyard into a full festival venue.

To our newest PNTCians: we are beyond thrilled to officially welcome you to our community! Your college adventure starts right now. Work hard, stay laser-focused, dream big, and soak up every single second of this journey.

"The voyage of a thousand miles begins with a single step." Welcome aboard, PNTCians! Let's make history together. ⚓
