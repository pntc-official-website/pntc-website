Celebrating Excellence and Endurance: PNTC Colleges Honors the Class of Talitha at Ringhop 2026

PNTC Communications·September 20, 2026

Years of early morning roll calls, late-night studying, relentless discipline, and unwavering determination all lead to one pivotal moment. For the students of PNTC Colleges, the Ringhop ceremony isn't just an event—it’s a rite of passage.

This year, PNTC Colleges proudly celebrates the Class of Talitha as they take this momentous step toward their future.

A Symbol of Commitment and Grit

The Ringhop ceremony marks a major milestone in every maritime and collegiate student's journey. Receiving and hopping through the ring signifies the transition from rigorous academic and practical training to stepping out into the professional world.

Every struggle overcome, every exam aced, and every practical exercise completed has built the foundation for this moment. The Class of Talitha has proven that with grit and dedication, no wave is too rough to ride out.

Wear Your Rings with Pride

To the Class of Talitha: your ring is more than a piece of jewelry. It is a badge of honor, a reminder of the bonds forged with your peers, and a symbol of the high standards of PNTC Colleges. When you wear it, you carry the legacy of resilience, professionalism, and excellence that defines your alma mater.

Steering Toward a Bright Future

As you place those rings on your fingers and take the symbolic hop forward, remember that this is only the beginning of your voyage. The horizons ahead are vast, filled with opportunities to lead, innovate, and make your mark in the industry.

Wear your rings with pride and sail toward a bright future, Class of Talitha! Fair winds and following seas on the journey ahead!

Congratulations to the graduates, their families, and the faculty who supported them along the way!
